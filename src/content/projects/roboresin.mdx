Control an entire fleet of robots by simply writing a prompt of what needs to get done, RoboResin will handle the rest.

This can be thought of as our implementation of a [Vision-Language-Action Model](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Vision-language-action_model) across a fleet of robots.

Check out the [devpost for RoboResin](https://devpost.com/software/roboresin), our HackTheNorth 2025 Project. 

Check out [RoboResin on Github](https://github.com/AJP-432/RoboResin)

## Features

- Access all robot information including live camera feed, sensor data, and live location through the RoboResin dashboard.
- Enter a prompt, and with standardized APIs across various robot makes, the LLM easily issues commands that can control the robots
- The result is a coordinated and optimized robotic response to any situation that may arise
